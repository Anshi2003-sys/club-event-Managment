Literature Club
Where words meet soul and creativity!

The Literature Club at Mizoram University celebrates the love for literature, creativity, and critical thought. We conduct poetry readings, book discussions, author talks, creative writing contests, drama, and debate competitions. The club welcomes anyone who enjoys literary activities, regardless of academic background!

  • Poetry and short-story competitions
  • Book review meetups
  • Creative writing workshops
  • Drama and debate sessions
  • Inter-university literary fest
