Red Ribbon Club (RRC)

Established: 2011

About the Red Ribbon Club

The Red Ribbon Club (RRC) is a preventive initiative to promote voluntary blood donation and create awareness about HIV/AIDS, mental health, life skills, and substance abuse among youth. It trains peer educators within the campus to spread awareness and inspire healthy living.

Established under the State AIDS Control Society (MSACS), the RRC at Mizoram University was formed on 24th June 2011 and officially launched by the Vice Chancellor on 6th September 2011.

Vision

To instill awareness on HIV/AIDS, life skills, mental health, and substance abuse, while promoting voluntary blood donation among youth.

Mission

• Spread awareness on HIV/AIDS
• Motivate youth to become Peer Educators
• Encourage voluntary blood donation
• Build responsible and healthy young citizens

Objectives

  • Address issues related to HIV/AIDS among students.
  • Spread correct and verified information.
  • Train Peer Educators and Change Agents.
  • Promote voluntary blood donation.
  • Encourage healthy lifestyles.

Major Activities

  • Peer Educators Training Programs (2011–2023)
  • Voluntary Blood Donation Camps (2012–2024)
  • World AIDS Day Observations
  • Mental Health Awareness Workshops
  • Red Ribbon Quiz Competitions (State Level)
  • Health Fest 2023 — Seminar & Workshop
  • Poster Making Competitions
  • Crimson Fest 2023 – Drama & Basketball (Runner up)
