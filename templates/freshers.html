Assam Freshers
Celebrating New Beginnings and Assamese Culture!
Event Date: October 11, 2025
Venue: Education Dept Seminar Hall1
Organized By:MZU Assam Association

Assam Freshers Night is an annual event at Mizoram University to welcome new students from Assam and highlight the beauty of Assamese heritage. The celebration features folk dance, music, ethnic food, and inter-batch games—strengthening the bonds of community, friendship, and talent.

  • Bihu and other Assamese folk dance performances
  • Welcome address and talent show
  • Assamese traditional food
  • Quiz competition and open mic
  • Interactive photo zones
  • Networking with club seniors and alumni
Bihar Freshers Night
Welcoming New Faces with Culture & Energy!
Event Date: October 18, 2025
Venue: Education Dept Seminar Hall1
Organized By: Bihar Association

The Bihar Freshers Night is a vibrant annual event at Mizoram University to welcome students from Bihar and embrace India’s cultural diversity. Dance, music, food stalls, and interactive games bring seniors and freshers together, fostering community and friendship!

  • Traditional dance & music performances
  • Welcome speeches and introduction rounds
  • Special Bihar Cuisine stalls
  • Fun contests, games & open mic
  • Photo booth & cultural dress showcase
  • Networking with club seniors and alumni
