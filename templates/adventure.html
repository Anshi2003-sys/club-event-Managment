🎯 Purpose of MZU YAC

The purpose of the Youth Adventure Club is to promote outdoor activities, build confidence and teamwork, encourage environmental awareness, and develop leadership, discipline, and fitness among youth through adventure and exploration.

🌄 About Us

The Youth Adventure Club (YAC) of Mizoram University is a student-led organization that promotes adventure, teamwork, and outdoor learning. We organize trekking, camping, cycling, and other adventure activities that help students build confidence, leadership, and a spirit of exploration.

Our mission is to encourage a healthy and active lifestyle while connecting students with nature and fostering unity through adventure.
